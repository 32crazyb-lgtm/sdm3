Before you apply

Admission essentials.

Admission is open to children of all backgrounds, subject to school rules. Application forms and fee information are available from the school office.

Entry requirements published by the school
Entry classPublished eligibilityDocuments
LKGAbove 3 years, 10 monthsBirth certificate, Aadhaar photocopy and four passport-size photographs.
Class IAbove 5 years, 10 monthsBirth certificate, Aadhaar photocopy and four passport-size photographs.
Class VIIIPassed Class VIITransfer certificate, progress card, Aadhaar photocopy, income and caste certificates if applicable, and four passport-size photographs.

Please confirm eligibility, current admission dates and document requirements with the school office before applying.

Helpful answers

For your family.

Contact the school ↗
Where do I get an application?
Collect the registration or application form, academic information and fee details from the school office.
When do LKG admissions begin?
The school’s published information states that LKG admissions begin in January. Contact the office for the current admission schedule and availability.
Which third languages are offered?
The published high-school language options are Hindi and Sanskrit.
Are additional photographs needed?
The school requests a recent child photograph and a joint photograph of the parents with the child on the application. Ask the office to confirm the required dimensions.
